The big idea

A statistic is itself a random variable because it changes from sample to sample.

Mean, median, variance, standard deviation, and range can each have their own sampling distribution.

The sampling distribution is not “the distribution of sample means.” The mean is only one possible statistic.

Things to try

Compare the sampling distributions of the mean and range from the same population.

Increase n while keeping the population fixed. Which statistics become more concentrated?

Switch to the skewed population. Do all statistics respond in the same way?
