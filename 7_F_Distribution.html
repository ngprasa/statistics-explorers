The construction
  1. Make one chi-square variable from v1 squared-Z contributions.
  2. Make another independent chi-square variable from v2 squared-Z contributions.
  3. Divide each by its own df.
  4. Take the ratio. That ratio follows an F distribution.
What to notice

F can never be negative because both numerator and denominator are non-negative.

It is generally right-skewed. Changing the two dfs changes the shape and how concentrated the ratio is around 1.

Key intuition: F compares two independent measures of variance.
