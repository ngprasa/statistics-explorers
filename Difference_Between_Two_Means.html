2. Sampling distribution of the difference

Each value is one repeated-sample difference, D = x̄1 − x̄2. The curve is the normal reference based on the theoretical mean and variance.

3. Standardized difference: Z or t

Conceptual chain: two populations → repeated samples → difference of means → sampling distribution → standardize. With known σ1 and σ2, the denominator is fixed and we compare with Z. With unknown σ1 and σ2, we estimate them with s1 and s2; the denominator is random, leading to a t reference under normal-population assumptions.
