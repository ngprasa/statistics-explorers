The construction
  1. Start with independent standard Normal values: Z₁, Z₂, …
  2. Square them: Z₁², Z₂², … . Every value is now ≥ 0.
  3. Add k squared values.
  4. The result follows a χ² distribution with k degrees of freedom.
What degrees of freedom do

Increasing k adds more independent squared Normal contributions. The distribution therefore moves to the right and becomes less skewed.
